# Sample Conversation — Website Development Agreement

Use this example to try DealSeal AI's conversation analysis. The parties begin with different price and delivery proposals, then explicitly resolve those differences and confirm all seven agreement categories.

**Final agreed terms:** $2,300 total; November 20 delivery; two revisions; $1,150 upfront and $1,150 after delivery. Scope, acceptance criteria, and IP ownership are specified below.

> **Client:** I need a website for $2,000, delivered November 15.
>
> **Developer:** I can do $2,300, delivered November 20.
>
> **Client:** $2,300 works. Two revisions included?
>
> **Developer:** Yes, two revisions included.
>
> **Client:** I agree to the November 20 delivery date.
>
> **Developer:** Confirmed. I will deliver the website on November 20.
>
> **Client:** I will pay $1,150 upfront and $1,150 after delivery.
>
> **Developer:** Agreed to the payment schedule.

## Scope of Work

**Client:** The website must include five responsive pages: Home, About, Services, Contact, and FAQ. It must work on desktop and mobile devices. The scope includes frontend development, a contact form, basic SEO setup, and deployment. Hosting fees and ongoing maintenance are excluded.

**Developer:** I agree to develop and deliver the five responsive pages, contact form, basic SEO setup, and deployment as described. Hosting fees and ongoing maintenance are not included in the $2,300 price.

## Acceptance Criteria

**Client:** I will accept the website when all five agreed pages are delivered, the contact form works correctly, the website displays properly on desktop and mobile, and there are no critical bugs. I will review the website within five business days of delivery and provide written acceptance or a list of issues.

**Developer:** I agree to these acceptance criteria. I will deliver a working website meeting the agreed requirements, fix any reported critical bugs within the agreed scope, and request written acceptance after the client's review.

## IP Ownership

**Client:** I will own the intellectual property rights to the custom website code, design, and deliverables created specifically for this project after full payment of $2,300. Third-party libraries, fonts, and licensed assets remain subject to their original licenses.

**Developer:** I agree to transfer ownership of the custom website code, design, and project deliverables to the client after receiving the full $2,300 payment. Pre-existing tools, reusable components, and third-party assets are excluded from this transfer and remain subject to their applicable licenses.

---

**Demo note:** This is a sample conversation for analysis and review, not a signed contract or authorization to charge a payment method. Both parties must review and expressly approve the final agreement.
